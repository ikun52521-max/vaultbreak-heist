How to play: move with WASD or arrow keys, or click anywhere to walk there. Grab loot with E when close; vaults take a few seconds to crack — stay out of sight while you work. Guards patrol fixed routes and chase when they spot you; cameras sweep their arcs. The alarm rises while you are seen and falls while you hide. Reach the glowing green exit once the target loot is yours. Every room has more than one route — the quiet one is rarely the straight one.
